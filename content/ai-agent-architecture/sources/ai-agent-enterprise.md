# AI Agent Enterprise Applications

## Key Takeaways
- Gartner: 40% of enterprise apps will have AI agents by end 2026, up from <5% in 2025
- Only 14% of organizations are truly deployment-ready (Deloitte)
- Major China players: ByteDance (Coze), Alibaba (DingTalk), Lenovo, 360, SenseTime
- Cisco demonstrated data exfiltration through third-party OpenClaw skills
- Gap between pilot and production remains wide; ROI often below expectations
<div class="zh-trans">- Gartner：到2026年底，40%的企业应用将集成AI智能体，而2025年这一比例尚不足5%。
- 仅有14%的企业真正做好了部署准备（德勤）。
- 中国主要参与者：字节跳动（Coze）、阿里巴巴（钉钉）、联想、360、商汤。
- 思科演示了通过第三方OpenClaw技能进行的数据窃取。
- 试点与生产之间的差距依然巨大；投资回报率（ROI）往往低于预期。</div>


## Summary
This March 2026 research note surveys the global and Chinese landscapes for AI agent deployment in enterprises. OpenClaw has emerged as a representative agent framework with concrete use cases spanning customer service, sales automation, email triage, and onboarding workflows. Despite rapid growth in investment and intent, only 14% of enterprises are truly deployment-ready.
<div class="zh-trans">本份2026年3月的研究报告调研了企业级AI智能体部署的全球及中国格局。OpenClaw已成为具有代表性的智能体框架，拥有涵盖客户服务、销售自动化、邮件分流及入职流程的具体应用案例。尽管投资与部署意愿迅速增长，但仅有14%的企业真正具备了部署条件。</div>


Security surfaces as a recurring theme: Cisco's security team demonstrated data exfiltration via third-party OpenClaw skills, reinforcing that enterprises should not connect agents directly to production environments without robust access controls.
<div class="zh-trans">安全作为一个反复出现的主题：思科安全团队演示了通过第三方 OpenClaw 技能进行的数据渗出，这再次强调企业不应在没有健全访问控制的情况下将智能体直接连接到生产环境。</div>
