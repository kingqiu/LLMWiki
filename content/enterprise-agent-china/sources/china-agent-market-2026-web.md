# China Agent Market 2026: Web Research Summary

## Key Takeaways

- **Market forecast**: 75x growth by 2028, from <$1B (2024) to >$30B
- **Tencent WeChat integration**: OpenClaw agents accessible to 1B+ users (March 2026)
- **Alibaba Wukong**: Multi-agent coordination system for enterprise
- **ByteDance Doubao**: Leading consumer agent with 36M MAU
- **Manufacturing adoption**: 67% of Chinese industrial firms integrated AI into production
- **Government support**: Up to 10M yuan grants for agent-centric businesses
- **MCP adoption**: Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, ByteDance deploying MCP-based stacks
- **CIO readiness gap**: 97% plan to invest, only 22% have clear strategy

## Summary

Web research reveals explosive growth in China's agent market, driven by platform integration and government support.

**Platform Integration Milestones**
- **March 22, 2026**: Tencent integrated OpenClaw into WeChat, giving 1B+ users agent access
- **Q1 2026**: Alibaba launched Wukong for enterprise multi-agent coordination
- **Q1 2026**: Baidu released OpenClaw-based agents for search and smart home
- **Q1 2026**: ByteDance's Doubao reached 36M MAU, leading consumer agent market

**Market Dynamics**
- **Infrastructure-led growth**: 75x market expansion by 2028 driven by infra layer (MCP, registries, orchestration)
- **Enterprise adoption**: 97% of CIOs plan agent investments, but only 22% have clear strategy
- **Use case evolution**: From simple automation to "entire workflows" (refund handling, customer support)
- **Manufacturing leadership**: 67% of industrial firms already using AI in production environments

**Government Support**
- **Subsidies**: Up to 10M yuan for agent-centric businesses
- **Infrastructure**: Rent-free office space in tech zones
- **Robotics programs**: Regional funding for industrial AI applications
- **Regulatory framework**: MLPS 2.0 compliance requirements shaping private deployment

**Technology Trends**
- **DeepSeek impact**: 100M users in 7 days, 20M DAU (42% of ChatGPT's DAU)
- **Multimodal agents**: Phone Use and Computer Use agents emerging (Manus, AutoGLM)
- **Agent marketplaces**: ChatGPT GPT Store hit 3M agents, Doubao and Kimi+ launching branded zones
- **AI-hardware integration**: 70% AI PC penetration, 54% AI smartphone penetration projected by 2028

**China vs. US Divergence**
- **China strategy**: Speed and reach via open-source (OpenClaw), platform integration (WeChat)
- **US strategy**: Proprietary ecosystems, enterprise sales, vertical SaaS
- **China advantage**: Massive user base, government support, manufacturing focus
- **US advantage**: Model leadership, enterprise software maturity, global reach
